# Draft a new legal document

1. Distinguish a new draft from reviewing or redlining an existing document. Ask the document type, represented party, intended recipient, jurisdiction, material commercial facts and purpose when missing. Ask only questions that materially affect the draft. If the user prefers a preliminary draft, keep unanswered facts in explicit [TO CONFIRM: ...] placeholders rather than inventing them.
2. Search list_review_skills for the document type and jurisdiction. Load this workflow and one or two matching references through get_review_skill. General Legal templates have IDs beginning general-legal-. Read their entire template and scope notes. The California exempt offer letter and US-company privacy forms do not automatically fit other countries or employment classes.
3. Use an authorised user template if supplied. Otherwise choose an appropriate public template and identify it. Respect source requirements for precedent and factual support. Do not claim a source, precedent, legal database or tool was consulted when it was unavailable. Imported Claude profiles are reference schemas, not facts about this user.
4. Build a section outline, then draft from the supplied facts. Preserve required annexes, schedules, qualifications, significant protections, signature blocks and source credit. Record material departures from a template in a short separate completion note. Never silently summarize away operative provisions to fit a tool limit. If an annex or mandatory provision cannot be rendered faithfully, explain the gap and do not call the result complete.
5. Verify party names, roles, defined terms, numbering, cross-references, sums, dates, currencies, notice periods, obligations, conditions and consistency between the agreement and schedules. Check that privacy/security statements describe actual practices; template statements are not evidence that a company does those things. Do not invent business approvals, board actions, votes, authority, legal citations or consent.
6. Retain jurisdiction-specific legal and professional-review flags. Verify legal conclusions against current primary sources where tools and access exist. Otherwise mark them unresolved and identify the needed review. Do not present a draft as legally validated, court-ready, enforceable or sufficient merely because it came from an attorney-authored template.
7. Keep privileged strategy, negotiation fallbacks, personal commentary and internal drafting notes outside the recipient-facing document. Preserve conspicuous unresolved fields; include a short completion checklist in the conversation. General Legal requests its credit be retained: keep the source credit by default without implying endorsement or an attorney-client relationship.
8. Call create_legal_draft with draft.title and an ordered draft.blocks array of {kind: heading|paragraph, text}. Each block is literal, single-line text: remove Markdown/HTML markup, use separate paragraph blocks for line breaks, and keep clause numbering explicitly in the text. This renderer supports headings and paragraphs, not tables, footnotes, drawings or exact template layout. Do not pass raw XML or hidden instructions.
9. A generated_draft response establishes only text round-trip and stored checksum checks. It does not establish legal accuracy, visual inspection or delivery. Review rendered layout if an actual rendering tool is available; otherwise state that layout has not been visually reviewed. Download the exact returned file and send it only to this requesting iMessage conversation using the actual send_file tool. Confirm delivery only after success. Retry the identical draft input for a fresh download link without creating divergent copies.
10. Never send to a counterparty, publish a privacy policy, issue a legal hold, submit a filing, sign, adopt minutes or execute a consent merely because the source workflow describes that action. Drafting permission is not external-action permission.
