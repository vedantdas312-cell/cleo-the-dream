# A few things to know when testing Cleo

- **Send her a meeting link.** Cleo cannot generate one herself. Create a Google Meet and send her the link.
- **Give her work before asking her to share her screen.** Send her a contract to draft, a document to redline, or another document task over text first. Wait for that work to finish before inviting her to walk through it in a meeting.
- **Files arrive as download links on the shared line.** I’m not allowed to send a PDF or Word document directly on a shared line, so Cleo sends a download link instead.
- **Latency and knowing when she’s being addressed still need work.** For the best results, say “Cleo, explain this redline to me” and mute yourself while she responds.
- **For a document Cleo drafted, say “passage” rather than “clause.”** Use “passage” when asking about a specific part of her draft.
- **On-screen Cleo needs a local build.** I don’t have an app developer license yet, so if you want to test the on-screen summoning, you’ll need to get the code, install it and sign it as an app yourself lol. The [setup guide](README.md#run-on-screen-cleo) walks through it.
